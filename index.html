About Family Nest

Family Nest Orphanage and Children's Home provides a safe and supportive environment for vulnerable and disadvantaged children in Kisumu, Kenya.

Through our charitable programs, we strive to meet essential needs including nutrition, healthcare, education, protection, safety and shelter while helping children build hope for a brighter future.

What We Provide

🍎 Nutrition

Helping children access regular and nutritious meals.

🏥 Healthcare

Supporting children's health and wellbeing.

📚 Education

Creating opportunities for children to learn and grow.

🏠 Shelter

Providing a safe and caring place to call home.

🛡️ Protection

Promoting safety, dignity and protection for vulnerable children.

❤️ Hope

Helping children look toward a brighter and more secure future.
